*Undergraduate course, SUTD*

## Description

An introduction to human-computer interaction with a focus on designing, building, and evaluating AI-powered interactive systems. Covers user research, interaction design, and prototyping methods, applied to the specific challenges of designing for AI: uncertainty, trust, explainability, and human oversight.
