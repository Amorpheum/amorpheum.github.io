I am an **Assistant Professor in the [Design and Artificial Intelligence](https://dai.sutd.edu.sg/) programme and [Information Systems Technology and Design](https://istd.sutd.edu.sg/) pillar at [SUTD](https://www.sutd.edu.sg/)**, where I lead the **[Context-Aware Interaction Lab](https://www.cailab.info)**.

My research is **Human-Centred AI**: designing and studying AI systems that people can understand, question, and act with, in sensitive, high-stakes settings where the cost of getting it wrong is borne by people, not systems. I build these systems with computer vision, audio, and natural language processing, and I evaluate them with the same rigour I bring to designing them: claims about what an AI system does for people have to be measured, not asserted.
