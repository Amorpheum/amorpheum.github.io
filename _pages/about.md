I am an **Assistant Professor in the [Design and Artificial Intelligence](https://dai.sutd.edu.sg/) programme and [Information Systems Technology and Design](https://istd.sutd.edu.sg/) pillar at [SUTD](https://www.sutd.edu.sg/)**, where I lead the **[Context-Aware Interaction Lab](https://www.cailab.info)**.

My research is **Human-Centred AI**: designing and studying AI systems that people can understand, question, and act with, in sensitive, high-stakes settings where the cost of getting it wrong is borne by people, not systems. I build these systems with computer vision, audio, and natural language processing, and I evaluate them with the same rigour I bring to designing them: claims about what an AI system does for people have to be measured, not asserted.

My work falls into three areas:

- **[AI in care and sensitive settings](https://www.cailab.info/research/#care).** AI agents that help people put difficult decisions into words in advance care planning, multimodal models aligned with speech-language pathologists on parent–child interaction (both CHI 2026 Honourable Mention Awards), and AI for mental health that supports peer supporters and caregivers. Throughout, I ask who carries the responsibility and emotional labour when AI joins a care relationship.
- **[Multimodal human–AI interaction](https://www.cailab.info/research/#multimodal).** AI that works across sketch, speech, video, and 3D: TalkSketch, which lets designers sketch and talk to generative AI at the same time; interfaces for modelling physical objects in virtual reality; and drone-based computer vision that gives swimming coaches real-time analytics.
- **[Responsible AI: risks and robustness of language models](https://www.cailab.info/research/#responsible-ai).** Where language models fail and whom those failures harm: how people use LLMs as both sword and shield in disinformation, how offensive-language detectors break under the cloaking and code-mixing of online speech, including in the low-resource languages of Singapore, and how well models understand cultures beyond the English-speaking world.

I'm always looking for motivated students. If this work interests you, see the lab's [Join Us](https://www.cailab.info/join-us/) page or drop me an email.
